How a job runs in FloorisPlan

From the first site visit to a signed contract — without leaving the app

1 Step 1

Project card

Client, address, and site notes in one place. Every job is a card you come back to — nothing is lost between the notebook, the tape measure, and email.

2 Step 2

Measure on site

Dictate dimensions by voice or send them straight from a Bluetooth laser meter. The floor plan and wall elevations build themselves as you go.

3 Step 3

Estimate

Quantities come from your measurements. Add works and materials from the catalog — or simply describe them to Floris AI and confirm the result.

4 Step 4

Contract

A DOCX contract with client details, scope, and total, with the estimate attached. Walk off site with a signature, not a promise.

Who is this app for

FloorisPlan is built for those who measure, price, and deliver renovation work

Contractors

Measure, price the job, and sign the contract in a single visit to the site.

Tilers

Wall elevations show the real surface — plan the tile layout before the first cut.

Electricians

Outlet, switch, and lighting layouts with exact mounting heights on every wall.

Plumbers

Water and drain points with precise dimensions, snapped to the walls of the room.

Interior designers

Measured surveys ready to hand over. Export to DXF and continue in your CAD editor.

Homeowners

Plan the renovation and see what it costs before the work starts.

Features

A complete toolkit — from the first measurement to the signed document

Floor plans with exact dimensions

Draw rooms by voice or by tap. Area and perimeter are calculated automatically, and snapping keeps the drafting precise.

Voice measurement

Say "line 500 right", "width 900" — no typing while your hands are busy with the tape measure.

Bluetooth laser meter

Pair a supported laser distance meter, press its button, and the measurement lands straight in the plan.

Wall elevations

Unfold any wall and see its full surface, with every outlet, switch, and water point in its exact position.

Electrical & plumbing

Outlets, switches, ceiling and wall lights, water and drain points, installations with mounting dimensions.

Construction estimate

Works and materials priced straight from the plan. Your own catalog, category totals, PDF export, 52 currencies.

Floris AI — job assistant

Say or type what needs to change: AI fills in the estimate, adds a client, a payment, a task, or a date in the calendar. 10 requests free, 200 requests per month with Pro.

Contracts in DOCX

Built from the project data. Use the standard template or import your own with placeholders — the estimate is inserted automatically.

DXF & PDF export

DXF opens in any desktop CAD editor, PDF is ready for printing and for the client. Export is free, with no limits.

Job schedule

Reserve the dates each job takes, with the client and booking status attached. Overlapping jobs are visible before you promise a start date.

Your professional profile

A business card for clients: photos of completed work and contact links — WhatsApp, social media, website. Up to 8 work examples.
