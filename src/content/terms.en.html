3. What the Application Does

The Application is a tool for renovation and construction professionals and their clients. Depending on the version, it lets you:

  • measure rooms and draw floor plans and wall elevations, including with a compatible Bluetooth laser distance meter;
  • prepare estimates, material lists, and change estimates;
  • keep project cards, tasks, notes, and work schedules;
  • generate documents from templates — contracts, annexes, subcontractor orders, invoices — and send invoices to the Polish National e-Invoice System (KSeF) using your own token;
  • use Floris AI, an assistant that answers questions and edits estimates and projects on your request;
  • export drawings and documents (PDF, DXF, DOCX) and share them through other applications.

The current set of features is described in the Application and on its Google Play page.
